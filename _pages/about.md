I am a robotics student who are interested in theory and real-world robot application.
Previously I have worked on some projects across different aspects in robotics from mechanical system, electronics, control, perception to reimplement the diffusion policy project.

My current interests are moving torwars **robot manipulation, dexterous hand, and tactile** to fully unleash the potential to work in factory or do housework at home. If happened, that will be exciting right?

In the past I completed my master in Dragon Lab under supervisor Moju zhao in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at **The University of Tokyo** and my bachelor in **Harbin Institute of Technology**(Harbin, China).
